# Beijing Four-Day Itinerary

Four days in Beijing is an ideal amount of time for first-time visitors to explore China's capital. You can visit iconic ancient landmarks, immerse yourself in modern creative districts, experience world-class theme parks, and take a scenic day trip to the Great Wall. This itinerary allows you to get the best out of four full days in the city under a budget of S$400 per person (excluding flights).

## First: A Few Things to Keep in Mind
The first and fourth days of this itinerary cover Beijing's major urban districts and cultural hubs, while Days 2 and 3 focus on day trips to Gubei/Simatai and Universal Studios. Traffic congestion in Beijing can be heavy, so switching to the extensive Beijing Metro system during peak hours will often save time and money.

Beijing is almost entirely a cashless city. Before arriving, download Alipay or WeChat (Weixin) and link your preferred debit or credit card to scan QR codes seamlessly. Keep transactions under RMB200 where possible to avoid extra merchant service fees.

## Itinerary Summary
**Duration**: Four days of 8 to 10 hours of touring per day
**Areas Covered**: Dongcheng District, Chaoyang District, Haidian District, Miyun District (Gubei), and Tongzhou District
**Sights visited**: Temple of Heaven, Yin San Dou Zhi, 798 Art District, Florist Cafe, Summer Palace, Gubei Water Town, Yongshun Dyehouse, Simatai Great Wall, Universal Studios Beijing (The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Transformers Metrobase, Kung Fu Panda Land of Awesomeness), Yin Ji Men Ding Rou Bing, Yuyuantan Park, Wudaoying Hutong, Siji Minfu, and Wangfujing Shopping Street

## Day 1: Beijing's Must-See Attractions

**6:00am: Temple of Heaven (天坛公园)**
Start your morning early at the Temple of Heaven, a UNESCO World Heritage Site dating back to the 1400s during the Ming Dynasty. Emperors used this ceremonial complex to pray for good harvests. Arriving at opening time (between 6:00 AM and 7:00 AM) is essential to enjoy quieter surroundings and beat the heavy tour crowds.
* *How to get there*: Alight at Tiantan Dongmen Station (East Gate of Temple of Heaven) on Metro Line 5, take Exit A, and enter through the East Gate.
* *Cost*: From RMB34/pax (~S$6).

**8:30am: Yin San Dou Zhi (尹三豆汁)**
Walk about 10 minutes from the North Exit of the Temple of Heaven to Yin San Dou Zhi, a Michelin-recommended local spot. Try *dou zhi* (RMB3), a traditional fermented mung bean drink known for its sour, pungent profile. It is a famous "acquired taste" in Beijing culture, but well worth sampling once for the experience.

**10:00am: Beijing 798 Art District (798 艺术区)**
Take a 45-minute DiDi ride northeast to the 798 Art District. This former 1950s military factory complex has been transformed into a creative district filled with contemporary installations, striking murals, art galleries, and quirky storefronts. Stop by one of the instant photography shops to take a mini polaroid souvenir for around RMB20–30 (~S$4).

**1:00pm: Late Lunch at Florist Cafe (花间 taste)**
Walk 8 minutes from the heart of the 798 Art District to Florist Cafe for lunch. Surrounded by colorful floral decor, this cafe serves popular dishes such as curry udon (RMB58) and light, fluffy souffle pancakes with strawberry sauce (RMB68).

**3:30pm: Summer Palace (颐和园)**
Take a 55-minute DiDi ride west to the Summer Palace, a sprawling UNESCO World Heritage site built in the 1700s during the Qing Dynasty. Explore its vast lakes, traditional gardens, and ornate pavilions. Wander toward quieter corners around late afternoon to capture photos of the Tower of Buddhist Incense as the golden hour light reflects across Kunming Lake.
* *Cost*: From RMB20/pax (~S$4).

**7:00pm: Hotel Check-In & Dinner**
Head to your accommodation, such as Manqu Hotel in Chaoyang District (near Wangfujing and the embassy area), to drop off your bags and enjoy a relaxed dinner at a local neighborhood eatery nearby.

## Day 2: Day Trip to Gubei Water Town and Simatai Great Wall

**9:00am: Travel to Gubei Water Town (古北水镇)**
Board a pre-booked day tour charter or tour bus from central meeting points like Guomao Subway Station or Shaoyaoju Subway Station (~S$62/pax including transport and guide). The drive northeast takes about 2 hours.

**11:00am: Explore Gubei Water Town & Sightseeing Cruise**
Stroll along the cobbled paths and stone cottages of Gubei Water Town, a recreated rustic water village at the foot of the mountains. Board a sightseeing boat cruise (from RMB80/pax) to glide along the canals. Be sure to visit Yongshun Dyehouse, a historical print and dye workshop established in 1900, to view vibrant printed fabrics drying in the courtyard and watch short local heritage performances.

**2:30pm: Cable Car to Simatai Great Wall (司马台长城)**
From Gubei Water Town, take the scenic cable car (from RMB120/pax round trip) up to the Simatai section of the Great Wall. Simatai is less crowded than Mutianyu but features steep, rugged terrain with dramatic views over the surrounding mountains. Note that cable cars operate uphill from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM and 5:30 PM to 8:00 PM.

**5:30pm: Golden Hour / Night View on the Wall**
Watch the sunset over the mountain ridges. Simatai is notably the only section of the Great Wall lit up for night tours, offering a unique illuminated night view before you descend via the evening cable car.

**7:00pm: Return to Central Beijing**
Meet your return bus or private charter at the Gubei Water Town parking terminal to travel back to downtown Beijing.

## Day 3: Universal Studios Beijing

**8:00am: Arrive Early at Universal Studios Beijing (北京环球度假区)**
Take a 30-minute DiDi ride from central Beijing to arrive at least one hour before the gates open at 9:00 AM. As the world's largest Universal theme park, queue lines for major rides build quickly.
* *Cost*: From S$90/pax.
* *Preparation*: Download the official Universal Studios Beijing app in advance to view live wait times and map out your path.

**9:00am: The Wizarding World of Harry Potter**
Head straight through the gates to Hogsmeade and Hogwarts Castle. Queue early for *Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey* to admire the detailed castle interior, moving portraits, and iconic props before the crowd expands. Grab a cup of non-alcoholic Butterbeer at the carts.

**11:30am: Transformers Metrobase**
Walk to Transformers Metrobase to ride the *Decepticoaster*, a high-speed coaster with multiple loops and drops. For lighter attractions, check out *Bumblebee Boogie* or the 3D dark ride *Transformers: Battle for the AllSpark*.

**1:30pm: Kung Fu Panda Land of Awesomeness & Lunch**
Head indoors to the fully shaded Valley of Peace inside the Kung Fu Panda zone. Enjoy a smooth boat ride on *Kung Fu Panda: Journey of the Dragon Warrior*. Dine at Mr. Ping's Noodle House for Hand Pulled Beef Noodles (RMB70) and a themed mochi dessert (RMB45).

**4:00pm–8:00pm: Exploration & Evening Departure**
Spend the remainder of the afternoon visiting other themed zones, capturing photos with characters, and browsing souvenir shops before returning to the city center via DiDi or Metro Line 7.

## Day 4: Cultural City Walk, Peking Duck & Shopping

**8:00am: Breakfast at Yin Ji Men Ding Rou Bing (尹記門釘肉餅)**
Start your morning with a filling breakfast of traditional Beijing meat pies (*men ding rou bing*, RMB7/piece). These thick, pan-fried pies resemble large soup dumplings packed with savory meat and hot broth. Pair them with stuffed meat pancakes (RMB28) and a dash of local vinegar.

**9:30am: Yuyuantan Park (玉渊潭公园)**
Take a 30-minute DiDi ride west to Yuyuantan Park. If visiting from mid-March to April, you can catch the Cherry Blossom Festival. Throughout the rest of spring and summer, the park features wisteria displays, scenic lakeside paths, and seasonal cherry-blossom-flavored drinks at park kiosks.
* *Cost*: RMB10/pax (~S$2).

**11:30am: Wudaoying Hutong (五道营胡同)**
Head to Wudaoying Hutong for a peaceful city walk. Unlike busier market streets, this narrow lane is lined with boutique shops, craft stores, and small cafes. Stop by Qing Zhi Hand Cream to browse floral-inspired creams, or grab a drink at Egg Coffee (Egg Coffee RMB22, Hong Kong Milk Tea RMB18).

**1:30pm: Peking Duck Lunch at Siji Minfu (四季民福)**
Head to the Wangfujing branch of Siji Minfu, a renowned Michelin Guide restaurant famous for roasted Peking duck (RMB118 / ~S$21 for half a duck). Wrap the crispy duck in thin wheat pancakes (RMB6) with sliced cucumber and spring onions.
* *Note*: Waiting lines can reach up to 3 hours during peak times. Arrive near opening time (10:30 AM) or grab a queue ticket early online/on-site and explore the nearby area while waiting.

**3:30pm: Wangfujing Shopping Street (王府井)**
Walk 5 minutes from the restaurant to Wangfujing Shopping Street, Beijing's famous pedestrian boulevard. Browse international shops, traditional craft stores, and local snack vendors. Book lovers can spend time inside the 10-storey Wangfujing Bookstore to browse regional literature and gifts before heading to the airport.

## Practical Tips for Beijing

**Getting Around**
* **Beijing Metro**: Highly recommended over taxis during peak morning and evening traffic hours. Fares start from RMB3 (~S$0.60). You can tap directly with a contactless credit/debit card at the gantry, or activate the "Beijing Rail Transit Ride" QR code inside the Alipay app under the Transport tab.
* **DiDi**: The primary ride-hailing service in China. Convenient for long-distance journeys or when traveling with luggage.

**Payments & Digital Setup**
* Set up Alipay and WeChat Pay with an international credit or debit card prior to arrival.
* Keep individual QR code payments under RMB200 where possible to avoid incurring additional transaction processing fees.
* Carry a physical credit card or debit card as a backup for large purchases at major venues or hotels.

**Essential Mobile Apps**
* **Navigation**: Download **Amap (高德地图)** as Google Maps does not function accurately in mainland China without specialized setups.
* **Translation**: Download **Pleco** or **Google Translate** (requires a working VPN) for reading menus and signage.
* **Lifestyle & Reviews**: Use **Dianping (大众点评)** to check restaurant menus, reviews, and opening hours.

**Attraction Guidelines**
* **Universal Studios Beijing**: Selfie sticks and tripods are strictly prohibited inside the park and will be confiscated at the security gate. Small and large storage lockers are available at the entrance for RMB20 to RMB40 per day.
